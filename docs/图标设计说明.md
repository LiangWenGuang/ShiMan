# 拾漫图标

使用内置 imagegen 生成，运行资源为 512 × 512 PNG；启动图标和应用图标保持一致。设计以翻开的漫画书、S 形书页、金色星芒和蓝青光带表达阅读与发现，不包含文字，便于缩小显示。

生成提示词：

Rich midnight navy and indigo background with tasteful electric cobalt and teal glow, subtle paper grain and dimensional soft shadows. Elegant contemporary editorial illustration, vivid but restrained, memorable silhouette legible at 48px, expressive manga energy without any character faces. Centered symbol occupies 65 percent with safe padding for HarmonyOS circular and squircle masks. No text, no letters, no watermark, no extra objects, no busy detail, no speech bubble. The output is the icon itself, not a device or presentation.
